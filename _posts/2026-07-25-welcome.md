Welcome 👋 This is the first post on my new site.

I plan to use this space as a lab notebook — short technical notes, longer write-ups when
I dig into something, and the occasional project retrospective. Topics will mostly orbit
**machine learning** and **embedded systems**, plus whatever else I happen to be building.

### Adding a new post

Posts live in the `_posts/` folder as Markdown files named
`YYYY-MM-DD-title.md`. The bit at the top between the `---` lines is the *front matter* —
it sets the title, date, description, and tags:

```yaml
---
layout: post
title: My next post
date: 2026-08-01 10:00:00+0530
description: a one-line summary
tags: machine-learning code
---
```

Everything below the front matter is just Markdown. Commit the file, push to GitHub, and
the site rebuilds and deploys itself automatically.

The [next post](/blog/) shows off the formatting features available — code, math, tables,
and more. Thanks for stopping by!
